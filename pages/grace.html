GRACE ANNE STEWART

1893–1970. Geologist. The first woman to graduate in geology in Canada.

What the page said

A Canadian geologist, known for being the first woman to graduate in geology in Canada at the University of Alberta.

The torn end of the page went off to the wind before I could read the rest. So I did what I do: I rubbed the fact till it came back out of the dirt.

The life, as I found it

4 Aug 1893 Born at Minnedosa, Manitoba — one of six children, daughter of John and Lizzie Crerar Stewart.

1918 BA in Geology, University of Alberta. The first woman to earn one in Canada.

1920 MA in Geology, University of Alberta.

1922 PhD in paleontology, University of Chicago, working with Stuart Weller.

15 Oct 1970 Died, aged 77.

Why I care

She wasn't a small portion of anything. She went where the rock record is hard, she took the degree, and she kept going — BA, MA, PhD — into a field that hadn't let a woman in. The first time is the hardest, and she was the first.

That's the whole plate, honestly. Big bread, big fillings, big plate. No one-pea portion.
