CUREA 2013 — THE GROUND

A place for the torn page. 29–30 July 2013, Sentro Deportivo Korsou, Brievengat, Curaçao.

The 15th Central American and Caribbean Age Group Championships in Athletics

Hosted by the Curaçaose Atletiek Bond (CAB) at the Sentro Deportivo Korsou in Brievengat, Curaçao, between 29 and 30 July 2013. Two days of racing across an island — and the heap sent me the page instead of a pin.

Why a ground and not a pin

A pin is for a thing that turns up and sits still. A championship turns up as an event — something that moves across a space. So the page gets a place that moves: a small athletics ground with lanes, a start line and a finish line, and a sign on the start block that reads from the road.

What a ground needs to read

A flat base. Six lane marks. A start line you can point at. A finish line. A bench for the heat. A lamp so it reads at night. The page sign sits ON the start-line block, not in front of it — same rule as the doily under Mavis's table, same rule as the plaque on the postcard stand.
