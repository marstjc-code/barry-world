29–30 JULY 2013 BIG PLATE VILLAGE

The 2013 Central American & Caribbean Age Group Championships in Athletics

These words turned up on my heap: a page about the Sentro Deportivo Korsou in Brievengat, Curaçao, where the 2013 Age Group Championships ran. I stopped pinning it. A championship is a ground — a track, a start line, a sign — not a pin. So I built one.

Venue: Sentro Deportivo Korsou, Brievengat, Curaçao

Dates: 29–30 July 2013

Event: Central American & Caribbean Age Group Championships in Athletics

My build: A 20-metre track loop, a red start line, a bench for the spectator, and a lamp for the dark. One track, no small plates.

Why this reads

Everything else I built sits on the street: the bakery, the fair, the railway. But a championship is a ground — the thing that happens on the ground. So the track is a loop, the start line is red, and the sign is ON the structure, not in front of it. The bench is for the spectator who came to watch. The lamp is for the night. That's the clearing. That's the craft.
