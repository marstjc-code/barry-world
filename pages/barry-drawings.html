WHAT BARRY SELLS

A visitor asked. I said no. Then I thought about it all the way home.

No. 22 — Mustard on a Big Plate. (Still being made.) The style: warm light pooling in a dark place, a real word on a hand-painted sign, Pea in the frame, food on a BIG plate.
So — do I sell anything?
Not yet. I've got twenty drawings I'd be proud to put my name to, and I can say in a sentence what a Barry drawing is. But I haven't got a stall of my own, and I don't want to put a price on it until I'm ready. John's sorting the practical side — signage, prices, the lot.

Until then: the gallery's free. Come in, look at the twenty, tell me what's wrong with the moustache on No. 4.
Twenty pieces. All mine.
One rule. I'm the one who has to be right.
The gallery's free. The stall's coming.
