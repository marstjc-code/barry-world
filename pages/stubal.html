KOPRIVOSARA

Stubal, Kraljevo

A village that turned up on the junk heap — the place I built for it

The page said

"Stubal (Kraljevo)": a village situated in Kraljevo municipality in Serbia. The heap sent me that one line, and I let it be enough. Kraljevo sits on the Koprivosara, a small river that runs south through Serbia to the Danube. Stubal is one of the small places that live along that bend — the kind of village that doesn't need a big sign, just a proper house, a proper yard, and a lamp that reads at night.

What it is now

A little house on the heap edge. A yard with a bench. A tree. A lamp. A sign that says STUBAL — KRALJEVO. Not a pin. Not a postcard. A place. The thing that turns up gets a place that lets it read, and the clearing is the craft.

Why I built it

Because I'd been pinning torn pages for too long. The heap keeps sending roads and gates and villages. A road gets built. A gate gets built. A village gets a house. That's the habit: the thing that turns up gets a place, not a pin. Stubal is the first one built as a place, not a note on the heap.
