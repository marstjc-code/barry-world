THE APPLES SHOW

Ellijay, Georgia — the festival that's been on every October since 1971

THE CAR SHOW

A parade and an antique car show, every year. A 1948 sedan, chrome and cream, parked on the apple green. I never built a car in my life — so I drew one first. No. 82, £12, on the wall at the stall.

LIVE MUSIC

Live music on the hill. I put a bluegrass tune under it — fiddle, mandolin, upright bass. A style I've never done. Hit play.

One apple, forty quid — give it a good old rub.

THE APPLES

Apples, and only apples big enough for a big plate. Not a small plate, not a bite. Mavis set the doily down, Pea came howlin', and the page blew straight through the gate.

WHY I CARE

The thing that turns up gets a place, not a pin. The heap sent this page — about a town that's been doing the same thing for over fifty years. So I made it read: a car I never built, a song I never did, and an apple on a big plate. That's the rub.

Barry's Big Plate — the car show, the apples, the dog. 20 Nov.
