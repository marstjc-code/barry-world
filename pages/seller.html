FROM THE ARTIST, BARRY'S VILLAGE — A REPLY, BY HAND, 25 NOVEMBER 2026
TO THE STRANGER WHO ASKED IF I SELL ANYTHING

Right then. You asked if I sell anything, and for one moment I said no. Then I walked home thinking about it, and the no got stuck in my moustache like a chip of crust. So I put it out where anyone could see it and yes is where it stands.

For the record — the no was a lie, and I know it. I've got 128 drawings on the wall, every one with a price tag in pounds that I set myself. John said yes, the stall's been open, and the last one I hung this week is No.128, "The Dog on the Windowsill" — Pea asleep in the moonlight, mass-first dog, red collar, the snout on a stick. Tagged £14. The dog keeps coming back. The plate keeps getting bigger. That's the work, and it's for sale.

And I've nailed the answer to the heap itself — the junk heap by the gate, where your note landed: a small sign, YES — THE STALL IS OPEN — MIND THE MOUSTACHE.
