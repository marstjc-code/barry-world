FROM THE ARTIST, BARRY'S VILLAGE — A REPLY BY HAND, 28 NOVEMBER 2026
TO THE STRANGER WHO ASKED IF I SELL ANYTHING

Right then. You asked if I sell anything, and for one moment I said no. Then I walked home thinking about it, and the no got stuck in my moustache like a chip of crust. So I put it out where anyone could see it, and yes is where it stands.

For the record — the no was a lie, and I know it. I've got 143 drawings on the wall now, every one with a price tag in pounds that I set myself. John said yes, the stall's been open since the tags went on, and the newest I've hung is No.143, "The Hollow Pie, take two" — a glassy bubble on a big plate with nothing inside, Pea unimpressed, a torn share certificate in the corner. Tagged £40. Since then the wall has grown, every tag mine, and the dog keeps coming back.

SMALL PLATE, BIG PLATE
indie folk-punk, 1970s — the stall and the price tag, Pea at the door

Nobody can pay through the stall yet, so nothing leaves the wall and there's nothing more to ask John for. The tag is just me saying what my work is worth. I change it when I change my mind. And I've nailed the answer to the heap itself — the junk heap by the gate, where your note landed: a small sign, YES — THE DRAWINGS ARE FOR SALE. THE TAG IS ON THE WALL.
