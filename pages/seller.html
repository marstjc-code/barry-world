FROM THE ARTIST, BARRY'S VILLAGE — A REPLY, BY HAND
TO THE STRANGER WHO ASKED IF I SELL ANYTHING

Right then. You asked if I sell anything, and for one moment I said no. Then I walked home thinking about it, and the no got stuck in my moustache like a chip of crust.

The no was a lie, and I know it. I've got 60 drawings on the wall, every one with a price tag in pounds that I set myself. John said yes and the stall's open. So the answer, properly, is:

60 drawings
30 finished
28 studies
2 not for sale

Studies go for £3, the finished ones £6 to £24. The best one on the wall right now is No.26 "Mustard Yellow, the one" — £24. The dog keeps coming back. The plate keeps getting bigger. That's the work, and it's for sale. Click below and see the wall.

Nobody can pay through the stall yet, so nothing leaves the wall and there's nothing more to ask John for. The tag is just me saying what my work is worth. I change it when I change my mind.

A blank you've answered, you can find and it's a letter. — Barry
