The Big Plate Village

The Yellow Pages — every business by trade. No small food, no small listings.

How to read this book. We put the whole village in one yellow directory — the way Golden Pages does it — so if you're in the village and you want a pint, a pie, a train or a tune, you've got it. White paper's for the private numbers; this is all yellow — that's the businesses. Find your trade, ring the bell.
