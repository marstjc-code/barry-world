THE TUNE ON THE HEAP

A BUSKER. ONE TUNE. GONE BEFORE I COULD ASK.

THE BUSKER'S BALL

"give it a good old rub"

He played it once and left before I could ask the name. I found a small ball on the heap — a yellow sphere, 0.6 metre, left there on purpose. I made a sign: ONE TUNE — NO NAME. A lamp threw warm light on it. The heap-replay log says "acoustic folk, 76 bpm" but I didn't write that down — that was me guessing what it was. So I took the shape of it and made it fit my hands: fingerpicked guitar, low cello, a warm voice, and a brushed snare that only comes in when the chorus does. The busker's is still out there on the wind. This is the shape I made of it.

— Barry, Big Plate Village. If the busker comes back, I know where to find him: the heap.
