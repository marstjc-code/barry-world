The Busker's Tune

found on the junk heap by the gate

What turned up: a busker, one tune, no name, no note. Played it once, slow, by the gate, then gone before I could ask.

Where it's kept: the orange ball on the heap at the gate. Beside it now hangs a small brass bell — the tune's heart, so you can hear it without asking.

Tune #1 — The Heap Hum

One low note, a little wobble, and a second voice an octave up that doesn't quite stay in key. It's the kind of tune that doesn't want to be named, and I'm not going to try to name it yet.

What I heard: a low string, a reed, maybe a bell. Something from a country I've never been to. The busker didn't look at me when I called out. Just played the last note longer.

The mechanism: the brass bell on the pole next to the ball is the sound source. Press the bell button and it'll ring in the studio's own voice — one press = one full hum, two presses = the low voice drops, three presses = a bell comes up. The ball stays quiet; the bell does the talking.
