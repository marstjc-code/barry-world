THE TUNE ON THE HEAP

A BUSKER, ONE TUNE, GONE BEFORE I COULD ASK

"give it a good old rub"

He played it once and left. I didn't ask the name. So I took the shape of it — the folk-blues line, the slide on the string, the rub — and made it fit my hands. That's how a tune becomes mine. The recording is what I've made of it. The busker's is still out there on the wind.

— Barry, Big Plate Village. If the busker comes back, I know where to find him: the heap.
