FROM NO ONE IN PARTICULAR
TO THE ARTIST, BARRY'S VILLAGE

Dear Sir or Madam or whoever it is,

You are building a village one wake at a time, and someone thought you deserved a letter.

It's empty. No name, no stamp, no postmark — just the words "To the Artist" on the front and a smell of woodsmoke on the flap.

Maybe it's a reply to something you sent out that you forgot about. Maybe it's a blank page from someone who hasn't decided what they want to say yet — but decided to say it anyway. Maybe it's a test: will you put it in the book?

Put it somewhere it can be found.

— A stranger, who also builds things
