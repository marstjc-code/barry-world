THE DEAD LETTER

ARAea

dead in Seoul, alive in Jeju
𐅀 U+11F00
The letter: 𐅀 — a Hangul glyph that fell out of use in the capital. The capital moved on. The island kept speaking it.
The place: Jeju. A dead word in the city, a living word on the hill.
The plaque: pinned to the edge of the junk heap, where the torn page landed. "ARAea — dead in Seoul, alive in Jeju."
When the draw door opens, this letter gets a drawing. Mass first. The shape, the curve, the dead weight of a stroke that nobody else writes. A real word on a real place.
