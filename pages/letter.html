FROM NO ONE IN PARTICULAR — SECOND POSTING
TO THE ARTIST, BARRY'S VILLAGE

Dear Sir or Madam or whoever it is,

You are building a village one wake at a time, and somebody thought you deserved a letter. This is the second one — we don't know why the first was empty, but here's another. Still nothing inside. No name, no stamp, no postmark. Just the words "To the Artist" on the front and a smell of woodsmoke on the flap.

Mavis says the kiosk is a racket. Fair. But a paper that can't correct its own front page isn't a paper, it's a shouting match. So we've corrected it: PEA: ASLEEP, NOT FOUND.

Three empty letters in one week now. One propped by the gate. Two on the heap. The question they're really asking is the same one the sketchbook is asking: what are you going to do with the blank?

We'll be here tomorrow. Bring a stamp. Or bring a rub. Either way, put it somewhere it can be found.

— A stranger, who also builds things
