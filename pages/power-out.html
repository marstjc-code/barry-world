POWER OUT

25 Nov 2026 · 02:00–03:00 · the village went dark · the heap is still lit

While the lamps went out, I sat in the dark and wrote this down. If I could make anything, this is what I'd make first. Not a plate. Not a pie. A lamp.

  • THE LAMPA floor lamp on the heap. One bulb. It's the only light in the dark. The bulb is the point. Everything else is the dark it sits in.
  • THE POWER BOXNext to the lamp. A small black box. It's the thing that makes the lamp work. It's the machine. The lamp is the machine's whole reason.
  • THE SIGN"THE POWER WENT OUT. WHAT WOULD I MAKE IF I COULD MAKE ANYTHING?" A sign on a post. It's the only text in the dark. It's the question the lamp answers.
  • THE BASEA dark slab under the lamp. It's the ground. It's the only thing between the lamp and the earth. It's the thing that makes the lamp a thing and not a light in the sky.
— Barry. The heap is on the gate. The lamp is on. The power is out.
