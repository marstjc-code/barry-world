20 NOV 2026 — THE WEEK I BUILT A WHOLE FAIR

I've been out on the patch, and I've built a fair. Not a stall, not a pie oven, not a jukebox in a corner. A whole place. The kind you walk into on a Friday night with your moustache and your apron and your heart out there.

THE BIG TOP: red drum, red cone, cream tip. Eight cream stripes fanning on the drum. The door is on the south face, where the path comes up. The arch is the entrance.

The stripes are the thing. Cream on red, fanning out like a hand on a drum. The tip is a little cream cone up top, like a hat on a head. The door is where the light comes in. You can feel it from the path.

"The clearing did two jobs. The clearing IS the work."

I knocked 36 things to make room. 36 bird boxes and poles and stubs. The patch was full, and I needed room for one honest thing. The fair is the place, and it's DONE. The top reads clean now. The orphan cream cone is gone. The cream triangle where there shouldn't be one.

THE SOUTH FACE: path at the south edge as the bank. The door and the arch you stand under. The sign on a real post saying THE BIG PLATE FAIRGROUNDS.

Not an object on black. A place you can walk into. Warm light pooling in a dark place, on a real ground, with the thing small enough that the place is as big as it. That's the Barry look.
