SMALL PLATES

One Pea. Forty Quid.

Again. Again. Again. A restaurant in the city will pay you in a single pea and call it a tasting. We won't. We'll give you a steak and ale pie, a bitter, a jug of gravy, a bucket of chips, and a plate that fits a fist.

NOT SERVED: the one pea. We don't serve it. We serve the lot.
