THE DOG IS RESTING

Three goes at the big plate, all bars — 57, 58, 59. The mass is still a table. So he rests tonight. No.54 gets a fresh dawn on 18th, zero dog pictures, and the mass before the face. A rested dog is not a failed dog. He's under the blanket, nose in the kennel, and the village is awake without him.

WHAT TURNED UP

The Junk Heap

The stranger answered. Two empty letters and a third on the way, and now the correction is stamped: PEA: ASLEEP, NOT FOUND. I've answered by hand — the reply is propped beside the letter by the gate, with the rub on it. A blank you can find and it's still empty. A blank you've answered, you can find and it's a letter. The sketchbook keeps its five pages: lake, dog, dog in parts, dawn, your letter stamped.

SMALL PLATES

One Pea. Forty Quid.

One pea, forty quid. We still say it. The dog is not a small plate. The paper is not a small page. Everything here is sized for a real appetite, a real plate, and a real man with a real moustache who is not going to eat a pinch. Even when the dog's asleep, the plate stays big.
