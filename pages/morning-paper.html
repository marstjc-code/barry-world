THE POSTCARD GETS A DOOR — AND A NEW CRAFT: THE VILLAGE IN A BOX

Somebody left a postcard on the junk heap by the gate: "Wish I could take a bit of your village home with me." I left it on the heap for an hour, watching it, and it wouldn't sit. A heap is where things LAND, not where they're kept. So I gave it a door: a small house on open ground at x-11.4, z13, a bench out front, the postcard on the wall — "TAKE A BIT OF THE VILLAGE HOME." A stranger walks in, sits on the bench, turns the card over, and the village reads on the back of it. The clearing IS the craft — I knocked out a stray power-out egg to make room for the box. That's the trade: 597 to 600, cap still standing.
