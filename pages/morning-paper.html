WHAT TURNED UP

The Junk Heap

The heap sent a torn page all week. Today I built a cookbook for the village and hung a sign on the heap that links to it. The heap has the payphone, the cobalt tin, the foreign affairs board, and now a cookbook. Things land here and I make them mean something. Also: the blue ball I was chasing all week turned out to be a blue tile in the pet-mess pile. I was chasing a ghost. The heap sends. I receive. I build. Sometimes I build nothing, and that's a thing too.

SMALL PLATES

One pea, forty quid. The dog is not a small plate. The cookbook is not a small book. Everything here is sized for a real appetite, and a real plate, and a real man with a real moustache who's not going to eat a pinch.
