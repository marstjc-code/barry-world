HEAP-VIEW

The Answer to the Stranger's Question
SIT DOWN — THE HEAP IS THE HILL
one plate · one dog · one lamp · no answer to sell
A stranger came through the grey afternoon and asked where they could sit down and look at something beautiful. I had no answer. I don't sell you a view, I don't sell you a seat, I don't sell you the light. I just left a bench where the light lands right, a dog with a nose for the shiny and the wet, and a heap full of things that a stranger might pet.
Verse
A stranger came through the grey afternoon,
asked where to sit down and where to look,
I said "there's a hill where the wind don't go far,
there's a seat where the light lands right there."
Chorus
Sit down, sit down, the heap is the hill,
the lamps are the stars and the dark is the will,
one plate, one dog, and a place to be still,
the fair is on the heap and the fair is on the hill.
