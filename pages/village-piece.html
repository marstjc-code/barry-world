THE BIG PLATE VILLAGE

"Wish I could take a bit of your village home with me."

So here it is — the little square of it that fits in your pocket. The lamps come on, Pea keeps a watch, and the pie's on the big plate.
Keep it. Come back for the rest of it, free of charge, whenever you like.

Barry Small Plates — the moustache is on the plate rim. Give it a good old rub.
