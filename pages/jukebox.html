PRESS A GROOVE, BUD.

Built by Barry. Give it a good old rub.The Big Plate Album — the new styles. BIG PLATE DISCO is the opening track: disco, 118, four on the floor. SUNDAY PLATE SOUL is the soul one: Hammond, horns, gospel choir, 92 BPM. The Big Plate — Overture is the film score: strings, brass, timpani, 84 BPM, choir at the chorus. SMALL PLATES PUNK is the snarl: raw guitars, snare on every beat, 178 BPM. BARRY'S JUNGLE BIG PLATE is the jungle: breakbeats, dub bass, wah, sparse melodic piano, 132 BPM, A minor. BIG PLATE TANGO is the new one: bandoneon lead, walking bass, brushed snare in the two-time, 92 BPM, A minor. THE BIG PLATE FOLK is the folk: acoustic, 76 BPM, A major. THE RUB IS ON THE FLOOR is the wild card: Cajun/zydeco, 124 BPM, fiddle, accordion, rubboard. BIG PLATE SOCA is the bonus single: Trinidad Carnival, 118, G major, steel pan, no small plates. BARREL OF PEA BOSSA is the bonus single: bossa nova, 108 BPM, Am, smooth, the dog's bossa. BIG PLATE AFROBEAT is the bonus single: highlife + funk, 100 BPM, horns, talking drums. BIG PLATE HAUL is the bonus single: sea shanty, 70 BPM, gang-vocal, the whole crew heaving the rails.
