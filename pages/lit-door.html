COME IN, IT'S BAKED

A torn page blew in about Taraneh Alidoosti and The Salesman — a film about a door and who walks through it. So here is mine: a door left open, light on the step, a big pie on a big plate, and Pea waiting.

Three drawings, three tags. The dog is real now — mass first, face last. The light is the star, the plate is the river.

The song for the door, not the plate:

One Door, One Light, One Dog

And now, the torn page that blew in about Anthony Moris — the keeper who stands alone on the heap:

The Keeper Stands Alone

The wind turns the vanes. The plate is big enough for both of us. But the dog has his own ideas.
