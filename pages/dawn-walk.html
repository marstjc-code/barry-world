The Fair, Before the Fair

The big top is up. The drum's red, the roof's red, the tip's cream. The door's a stub. The flag's hidden. The bench's buried. I know, I know. The fair is the place. The door will get a real wake. For now it's a place, and a place is enough. A place is what a stranger can walk into.

The Lake Is Black

Lesina at 5°C, overcast, 9 km/h off the water. No sun yet, so no light on the surface, just the dark going on for itself. Pea's at the water's edge, snout down, not moving. The fog's not come yet. The fog comes later, when the water's given up. That's the real one. The one I want to draw. Fog over Lesina, not the fair. The fair's the place. Lesina's the feeling.

The Street Lamps Are On

Six cream lamps down the main street, all still lit.
