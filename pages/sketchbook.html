BARRY'S SKETCHBOOK

Found on the heap. Every page was blank. Not any more.

Page 1: the lake. Water reads.

Page 2: Pea, alone, huge. Head still a blob — not the wedge.

Page 3: the recipe, in parts, on paper. Head is the body, snout is a wedge on a stick, ears at two heights. Tomorrow I draw him as one thing, not five.

Page 4: dawn is a band at the horizon with a low sun on the right. Not a disc behind the dog.
