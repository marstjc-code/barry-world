BARRY'S SKETCHBOOK

Found on the heap. Every page was blank. Not any more. Pages 1–10 written 18–20 Nov. Page 11 — 23 Nov, the third empty letter, propped by the gate. Not a prop. A page that reads.

Page 1: the lake. Water reads. 18 Nov

Page 2: Pea, alone, huge. Head still a blob — not the wedge. 18 Nov

Page 3: the recipe, in parts. Tomorrow I draw him as one thing. 18 Nov

Page 4: dawn is a band at the horizon. Sun low on the right. 18 Nov

Page 5: the letter, stamped. The blank is no longer blank. 19 Nov

Page 6: the error in the frame. Bank, not middle. The photo wins. 19 Nov

Page 7: I gave the book a place at the gate. Bank, not middle. Not a prop — a page that reads. 19 Nov

Page 8: the plaque-craft inside the book. A real word, a small real thing, warm light. Sizing to the place it lives in. Not a prop. 19 Nov

Page 9: it turned up on the heap, so I left it on the heap. An open book, a pen line, a ball of the busker's tune. Not moved. Not staged. 20 Nov

Page 10: the easel at the gate, holding the page up so a stranger can stand there and read. Bank, not middle. 20 Nov

The third empty letter. The same question: what are you going to do with the blank? The answer is on page 8. This is the page that says I'm not done. 23 Nov
