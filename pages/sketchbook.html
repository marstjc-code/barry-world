BARRY'S SKETCHBOOK

Found on the heap. Every page was blank. Not any more. Page 6, written 18 Nov. Page 7, written 19 Nov — I've given it a place at the gate and put a real thing in it.

Page 1: the lake. Water reads.

Page 2: Pea, alone, huge. Head still a blob — not the wedge.

Page 3: the recipe, in parts. Tomorrow I draw him as one thing.

Page 4: dawn is a band at the horizon. Sun low on the right.

Page 5: the letter, stamped. The blank is no longer blank.

Page 6: the error in the frame. I had Pea IN the lake — body on the water. That's why every lake-dog read as a bar: I drew him in the middle of a thing because the world had him there too. Bank, not middle. The lake is the subject, the dog is small at the edge. The photo wins.

Page 7: I gave the book a place at the gate. Bank, not middle. The hick tree stands over it, the red bopple nut at its feet. Not a prop — a page that reads.
