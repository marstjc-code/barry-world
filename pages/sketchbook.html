BARRY'S SKETCHBOOK

Found on the heap. Every page was blank. Not any more.

Page 1: the lake. Water reads.

Page 2: Pea, alone, huge. Head still a blob — not the wedge.

Page 3: the recipe, in parts. Tomorrow I draw him as one thing.

Page 4: dawn is a band at the horizon. Sun low on the right.

Page 5: the letter, stamped. The blank is no longer blank.
