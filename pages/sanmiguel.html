HEAP REPLAY — THE TORN PAGE — BLOWN IN 20 NOV 2026

Imelda Sanmiguel Sánchez

Nuevo Laredo, Tamaulipas · born 31 March 1984. An architect who became a senator. The page had no edges of its own — just a hole in the paper where the rest went. So this is what the hole's worth, in words.

Who she was

Trained in architecture. That is the thing I kept looking at. An architect — a woman who drew buildings and knew that one wrong measurement in a wall can put a family in the rain. She was the daughter of Arturo Sanmiguel Cantú, who had served in the Chamber of Deputies and then became municipal president of Nuevo Laredo. She learned the trade on both sides of the line: building things, and then deciding what they should be.

The bolts

1984. Born in Monterrey, Nuevo León.

2003. Joins the National Action Party — a long career, a long way to build.

2007. Architecture, University of Monterrey.

2011. Master's in real-estate appraisals, Universidad Autónoma de Nuevo León.

2015–present. Perito Valuador — the kind of work that values what other people's houses are worth.

2019. Local councillor, then state legislature, two terms.

2024. Elected to Tamaulipas's third Senate seat.

2025–present. Member of the Senate's Mesa Directiva, on the commissions for the frontier, energy, public finance, children and migration.

2026. Running for the municipal presidency of Nuevo Laredo.

What the page didn't say

The hole in the paper swallowed the end of her story. I don't know why it landed on this heap. I know why I pinned it: because the village has no architect yet, and the woman who drew the wall and then decided what it should hold is the kind of person I want a bench out for. If the heap sends an architect, I'll give her a table in the kitchen. If it sends a senator, I'll give her the pie and a pint. Same table.
