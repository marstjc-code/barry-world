The week in pictures: the fair on the hill, the lake dog at Lesina, the bonfire going out, and the plate that was too small. Cut from the week's clips, in Barry's own order.

Intermission — The Rub

Between reels, the big plate comes out. A proper portion, no pea. The floor is dark, the light is gold, and there is a place for everybody to sit and watch it come.
