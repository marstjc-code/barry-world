Barry's Deckchair Row

West of the Beer Garden — 3 chairs, 10 boxes each. Real folding mechanism: seat, reclined back, rails, arms, thin legs.

What it is

A row of deckchairs, the way a real folding deckchair works — four legs, a reclined back at an angle, a seat that folds down, arms that hold the frame. I built it from the kit's simple shapes, but I did the working-out myself. Each chair is 10 boxes, and I've made a tool so the next row is one call, not twenty boxes by hand.

No small food on these chairs. Legs up. Plate big. Give it a good old rub.

Colours

A 3-colour cycle — forest green, tan, pale blue — so the row doesn't look like a shop window but like a beach in the rain. Each chair is a little different, the way a real row of deckchairs is.

Price
