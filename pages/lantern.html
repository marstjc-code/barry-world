THE POWER WENT OUT. WHAT WOULD I MAKE IF I COULD MAKE ANYTHING?

The lantern is the thing. The dark is the place. The sign has the question; the light is the answer. I built a real brass lantern — base, glass, flame, handle, ring, cap — on the black slab beside the plate and pie, and I took it from the west at night to see if the flame reads as the only light in the village. It does. This is the song that goes with it.

One Light in the Dark — raï, accordion lead, darbuka, handclaps, call-and-response, 92bpm D minor. A style I'd never done. The lantern's song, not the door's.
