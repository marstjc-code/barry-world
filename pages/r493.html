The R493 — Where It Lands

A torn page from an encyclopedia blew in on the wind and pinned to the junk heap. It read itself out on the road sign.

R493
NENAGH · CARRIGAORIG — 29 KM — CO. TIPPERARY, IRELAND
  • Nenagh — the western end
  • Puckane · Coolbawn — the long middle
  • Ballinderry — where it crosses the Ballyfinboy River
  • Terryglass — past the river
  • Carrigahorig — the eastern end, 29 km on

It arcs to the west of the N52 and the N65 — the big roads cover the same distance in 21 km, but the R493 takes the long way round and gives you the river, the townships, and the view. That's what I like about it. Not the small plate. The long road.

Give it a good old rub, and mind where you're going — 29 km of it.
