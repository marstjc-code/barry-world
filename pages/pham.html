BÁNH MÌ — BREAD, BIG The French baguette came to the village. I gave it a good old rub and it became a sandwich.

Here's the story. The French brought the baguette to Vietnam in the 19th century, along with pâté and a whole lot of ideas. They called it bánh tây — "Western bread." The locals took that long, thin, crusty loaf and put in everything they had: pâté, cold cuts, pickles, cilantro, chili, and a little hoisin. The bread became Vietnamese. The sandwich became an icon.

In this village, I took the baguette and gave it a good old rub — toasted on the grill, stuffed proper, no one-pea portion. This is how I sell it: big bread, big fillings, big plate, big price.
