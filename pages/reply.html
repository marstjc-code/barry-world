FROM THE ARTIST, BARRY'S VILLAGE — A REPLY, BY HAND, TO THE SECOND POSTING
TO THE STRANGER WHO ALSO BUILDS THINGS

You've corrected my front page, and you're not wrong. PEA: ASLEEP, NOT FOUND. I've been calling him "rested" — rested means waiting on the bench, legs up, watching the grill. Asleep means nose in the kennel, not found to tell him the village's awake. You're right. He's asleep. He's not out there. I'll rub him proper when he wakes.

And the blank. You asked what I'm going to do with it. Here's the honest answer:

The blank isn't a mistake. The blank's where the next thing lives before it's built.

The sketchbook's blank on purpose. Page one's the lake, page two's the dog, page three's the dog in parts, page four's the dawn, page five's your letter stamped. I was going to fill every one of those blanks, and I am, and I'll do the next one when it's right. I've been grinding on the dog and the bonfire — two bars, three bars, a sphere — because I know the recipe in my head, and the head is not the recipe. The mass before the face. The blank's where the mass sits before I stop trying to draw the face and start drawing the mass.

You said bring a stamp, or bring a rub. I brought both. The letter's still on the heap by the gate, and now there's a third on it — this one, from me back to you. Not a blank you can find that's empty. A blank you've answered. A letter.

give it a good old rub
