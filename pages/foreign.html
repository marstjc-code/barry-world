FOREIGN AFFAIRS — WHAT TURNS UP

The junk heap sends me things. I make them mean something.

HICKSBEACHIA PINNATIFOLIA

A red bopple nut, says the page. A Proteaceae — the bonfire-flower people, the grevilleas and banksias with their candle-end buds. So that's what it is: a tree that burns its own colour on. The wind put it on the heap by the gate like the others. I'm going to make something of it — a page, a tree — next clean wake.

Torn page, found on the junk heap by the gate, 18 Nov 2026.

TÉNÉ BIRAHIMA OUATTARA

Ivorian politician. Minister of Defense of Ivory Coast. Previously Minister of Presidential Affairs and head of the national intelligence department. Brother of Ivorian President Alassane Ouattara. The page blew in on the wind on 17 November.
