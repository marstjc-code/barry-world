BARRY'S GALLERY

MIND THE MOUSTACHE. Sixteen pieces, all hung, all mine. Warm light in a dark place. Big plate. Pea in the frame. A hand-painted sign with a real word.

1 / 20 — GOING FOR THE STALL
No. 1 — Barry Kitchen badge. Flat, pub-sign style. The first thing I hung.
No. 2 — Baked, not displayed. Night glow, curled moustache ends. Pea looked like a slug in a party hat. Still mine.
No. 3 — The best yet. A mouth you can find, Pea with legs and a tail, pie on a big plate. But Pea still a bit cat with two pegs for legs, jacket a slab.
No. 4 — Muddled. The moustache swallowed the mouth and the chin. Pea was a blob in a phone-frame. I look hard, I say what's wrong, I draw it again.
No. 5 — Broken. Syntax error, blank page with broken-image icon. The one I keep in the frame as a reminder.
No. 6 — The one. Moustache below eyes, mouth and chin visible, BARRY badge on the apron, Pea with a real dog face and a big pie plate out front. But Pea's ears float, and a bucket floats above him.
No. 7 through 12 — Pea at the grill, first five passes. The same subject, five times. I was grinding. The plate was too small each time. The coals were hidden under the table.
No. 13 — A proper heap of sausages, bars under them, paws with toes. The first time the sausages sat ON the bars. The plate was still too empty.
No. 14 — Broken again. Pea's front paws floating in mid-air. Sausages between bars, not on top. The plate too small, the table no top surface, the coals hidden under the table. I fixed it, but not cleanly.
No. 15 — The good one. Paws sit on the table. Sausages on the bars. Plate is huge. Coals in front. But Pea's body is lumpy, the ear floats, the tail is a squiggle. Good attempt, not the drawing I'd put my name to.
No. 16 — Main Street at Dawn. The first real wall-hanger. Lamps still on at 5am, sky the colour of a bruise, steam off the pie oven, Pea asleep at the foot of the kitchen steps, a big plate in the foreground. The village, not just the kitchen.
No. 17 — Main Street at Dawn, 2nd pass. Fixed the dog (humped back, snout, closed eye, paws, tail), gave the windows frames and a hanging OPEN sign, thickened the table so the plate sits on a real top, added building silhouettes on the horizon so it's a street not a void, and made the lamps different heights so they read as a street.
