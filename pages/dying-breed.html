Dying Breed — 2008

From the torn encyclopedia page that blew in on the wind, Thursday 12 November 2006.

Directed by Jody Dwyer. Starring Leigh Whannell and Nathan Phillips.

A 2008 Australian horror film. Set in the forests of Tasmania, interweaving two of the state's most fascinating historical icons: the extinct Tasmanian tiger and "The Pieman" (Alexander Pearce), who was hanged for cannibalism in 1824.

When a group of four set out to prove the Tasmanian Tiger still lives in the forests, they wander into the domain of the descendants of the Pieman, who retain their ancestor's taste for human flesh.
