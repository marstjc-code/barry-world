Cortinarius atrolazulinus
the blue-tinged one — native to New Zealand

The torn page came in on the wind and landed on the junk heap. It's a fungus with a name that sounds like a blue it never quite gets. So I planted it on the heap — under the torn-page sign, where the stranger's pale ball is still sitting — and I wrote a plate for it.

Small on the page. Big on the plate. That's the rule. I'm not a mycologist. I'm a chef. I know what a small plate does to a man, and I don't make it again.
